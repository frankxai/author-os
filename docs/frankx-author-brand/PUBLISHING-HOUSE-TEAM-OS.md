## Definition of cool (non-negotiable)

A FrankX book is cool when:

1. **One sentence only Frank would write** appears in the first 400 words  
2. Cover is **flat graphic**, big type, series DNA — not orb, not 3D book photo  
3. Reader can **run an experiment within 7 days** (Book Two always; Book One via living-the-law)  
4. No banned AI sludge (voice guardian ≥ 100 clean)  
5. Transparent craft note: how agents + humans made it  
6. Links to **living systems** (journal, agents, music, CoE) without sales cringe

- [ ] Mechanism without jargon fog  
- [ ] Frank scar or studio scar (where true)  
- [ ] No premature naming in Book One  
- [ ] Practice / inhabit instruction  
- [ ] Ban-list clean  
- [ ] Internal link to series / companion where relevant
