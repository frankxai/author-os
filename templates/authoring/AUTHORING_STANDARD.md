# Authoring Standard

This standard governs public and strategic writing for the project.

## Core Principle

Write like a serious human with taste, evidence, emotional intelligence, and strategic clarity. The work should be useful to readers, legible to agents, and strong enough to survive expert review.

## Required Qualities

1. **Truth before style**
   Do not make factual, legal, technical, medical, financial, pricing, or performance claims without support.

2. **Specificity before abstraction**
   Prefer concrete nouns, real mechanisms, exact promises, and visible proof.

3. **Depth before hype**
   The writing should reveal judgment, synthesis, and lived understanding.

4. **Voice before polish**
   Preserve the project voice. Do not flatten strong writing into generic assistant prose.

5. **Invitation before pressure**
   Use attention, story, contrast, and challenge ethically. Do not use false scarcity, shame, fear, fake proof, or manipulation.

6. **Structure before decoration**
   Every section should have a job. Every transition should move the reader.

7. **Human approval before locked-state change**
   Locked files, legal claims, positioning, pricing, canon, and final public promises require approval.

## Writing Rules

- Lead with the real subject, not throat-clearing.
- Use rhythm: vary sentence length and weight.
- Delete empty intensifiers and generic praise.
- Replace vague transformation words with concrete before/after states.
- Make the reader feel seen without pretending to know private facts.
- Use stories and epiphany moments when they clarify meaning.
- Keep paragraphs short enough to scan but not so fragmented they lose thought.
- Avoid cliches: seamless, unlock, elevate, revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, delve, robust.
- Avoid manipulative conversion language.
- Keep public promises narrower than internal ambition.

## Agent Rules

- Read `AUTHORING_PROJECT_BRIEF.md` before rewriting.
- Read locked files before editing strategic/public copy.
- Start with inventory or critique unless the user explicitly asks for a rewrite.
- Produce diffs or proposals for material changes.
- Add unresolved risks to the report.
- Do not publish or upload.
- Do not mutate locked files unless explicitly instructed.
