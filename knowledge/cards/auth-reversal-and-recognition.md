# Reversal and recognition are plot parts

Peripeteia and anagnorisis are not spice. They are structural organs.

**Agent use:** chapter briefs should mark at least one reversal or
recognition per act, or explicitly waive it.

Orthography in the Gutenberg HTML is slightly broken (“emotional:
interest”); the mechanism is still clear.
