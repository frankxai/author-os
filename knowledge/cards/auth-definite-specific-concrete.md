# Concrete language

Strunk lists this as rule 12. The TOC line is the extracted artifact;
the full examples remain in Gutenberg #37134.

**Agent use:** in scene drafts, require at least one sensory particular
per beat. “He felt nervous” is a fail; a dry mouth, a delayed reply, or
a named room is a pass.

Later Strunk & White editions are **not** this card.
