# Arcanea Author Cockpit Scene Brief

## Surface

Route: `/projects/[id]/cockpit`

The first viewport is the product, not a landing page. It opens on a live author cockpit with manuscript spine, visual story surface, inspector, agent rail, and publishing ops strip.

## Static Composition

- Left: manuscript spine and progress.
- Center: story canvas, corkboard, relationship graph, timeline.
- Right: selected codex entity, DAM strip, agent task board, continuity status.
- Bottom: offer, model routing, and publishing readiness strip.

## Motion

V1 uses stillness and stable hover/focus states only. Motion job for later: reveal evidence links between canvas, scene cards, timeline, and codex without interrupting writing.

## Asset Policy

No external or generated assets are shipped in V1. Character and mood assets are represented as provenance records in the DAM so the generated-media gate can be applied later.
